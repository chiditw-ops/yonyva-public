Ko Vault Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated November 19, 2026. Chidi International Limited provides the Ko Vault Android app (com.chidi.kovault). Contact us at chidi@88.com.

1. Data stored on your device

Files, card details and attachments, accounts and transactions, notes, passwords, and photos that you add are stored in the app's private space on your device. Vault content is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with the encryption key protected by Android Keystore. Ko Vault has no developer cloud account or server vault sync. We cannot recover local content from a server copy.

2. Access and permissions

The local PIN controls access to the app and is not the encryption key. Optional biometric authentication is handled by Android. The app does not receive fingerprint or face templates. System pickers let you choose imports and backup destinations. The camera scans transfer QR codes. Nearby Wi-Fi permissions support device discovery and connection. Older Android versions may require location permission for Wi-Fi functions. These functions do not create a location history.

The file vault can hold APK files you choose. When you open one, Android's installer controls installation permission and confirmation. The app does not install packages silently or download update packages on its own.

3. Network activity and other services

When you manually update exchange rates, the app requests rates over HTTPS from open.er-api.com by default, or from an HTTPS provider you configure. Vault transactions, passwords, files, and photos are not included in that request. The provider receives the request and network information such as IP address and applies its own retention rules. See ExchangeRate-API terms.

When you choose to support the developer or check an order, Google Play Billing handles product queries, purchase state, purchase tokens, and consumption acknowledgments. Support is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks no features or subscription. The app does not read your payment card number. Google may receive transaction records, IP address, and Billing diagnostic information such as results, errors, timing, app and SDK versions, random session ID, device model, OS, language, time zone, and network type. We can view sales, refund, and settlement records provided in Play Console. Google controls retention and deletion of its records. See Google Privacy Policy.

For user-initiated local Wi-Fi transfer, the password-protected encrypted backup goes to the device you select. Connection tokens, length, and acknowledgment metadata are outside HTTPS/TLS protection. Use trusted devices and networks. The app currently shows no ads. Opening website links uses your browser and may expose network request information to the site host and browser.

4. Backups and deletion

Deleted in-app items stay in the recycle bin for seven days for recovery. They expire after that period and the app removes them during cleanup. You can permanently delete them earlier. You control external backups and where they are saved. Exported backups and copies can remain after in-app deletion or uninstalling and must be deleted from their destination separately. A cloud storage provider you choose handles the backup under its own terms. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Without a usable backup, content lost through device loss, app data clearing, or uninstalling may be unrecoverable.

5. Sharing and widgets

Recipients receive content you choose to export, share, or open in another app. Some exports can be unencrypted. Clipboard content and home-screen widgets may be accessible outside the vault. Check the destination and content before sharing.

6. Contact and changes

If you email us, we receive your address, message, and attachments you send, and use them to answer you. Do not send a PIN, password, or sensitive file. You may request information about or deletion of messages we receive. Manage vault content on your device. We update this page and its date when processing changes. Contact chidi@88.com.
