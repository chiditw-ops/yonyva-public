Ko Vault Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated November 19, 2026. Chidi International Limited provides the Ko Vault Android app (package name com.chidi.kovault). For privacy questions, contact chidi@88.com.

In brief: Vault content is stored locally on your device and encrypted. Ko Vault does not provide a developer cloud account or server-based vault sync. Optional exchange-rate updates, developer support and order checks connect to their respective third-party services. Local Wi-Fi migration sends a password-protected encrypted backup to the device you select.

1. Content stored on your device

Files, card details and attachments, accounts and transactions, notes, passwords, and photos that you add to Ko Vault are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Vault data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM, and the encryption keys are protected by Android Keystore. The app does not sync vault content to a Ko Vault account or a developer-operated server. We therefore cannot restore local vault content from a server copy.

2. PIN, permissions and device access

Your local PIN controls access to the app; it is not the encryption key for vault content. Optional biometric authentication is handled by Android. The app does not receive fingerprint or face templates. Device capabilities, Android settings and permissions you grant determine whether optional features can be used.

You choose files to import and backup destinations through Android system pickers. The camera is used to scan a device-migration QR code. Nearby Wi-Fi permissions support device discovery and connection; older Android versions may require location permission for Wi-Fi functions. These functions are not used to create a location history.

The file vault can store APK files that you select. When you choose to open an APK, Ko Vault passes it to Android through a read-only content URI. Android's installer handles any unknown-source permission and the final installation confirmation. The app does not silently install packages or download update packages on its own.

3. Network activity and third-party services

Manual exchange-rate updates. Only when you tap the update control, the app requests exchange rates over HTTPS from ExchangeRate-API at open.er-api.com by default. You may instead configure your own HTTPS endpoint. The request does not include your vault transactions, passwords, files or photos. The service provider can receive the request and network information such as your IP address. The default open-access endpoint applies IP-based rate limits: an IP address that receives HTTP 429 responses can make requests again after the rate-limit period ends, which the provider documents as 20 minutes. This is a rate-limit reset period, not a statement about how long request logs are retained. Other retention and deletion practices are governed by the provider's terms and privacy policy. See ExchangeRate-API terms and privacy information. If you configure another provider, that provider's own privacy terms apply.

Developer support and order checks. Only when you choose to make a support purchase or check an order, the app connects to Google Play Billing to query products, retrieve purchase status and purchase tokens, and submit consumption confirmations. Support is an optional, repeatable one-time purchase; it is not a subscription and does not unlock extra features, membership or badges. The app does not read your payment-card number. It stores only a local boolean marker that a support attempt was made, to help recover an interrupted payment; it does not save purchase tokens in its preferences. Billing queries are not automatically started at app launch or when the app returns to the foreground. The Google Play Billing Library may send Google technical diagnostics about billing operations. Depending on the library version and operation, these may include request results, errors and timing; the app package, app and Billing Library versions; a random session identifier; and device or environment details such as device model, Android version, language, time zone and network type. Google also receives network information such as the IP address as part of the connection. These diagnostics support the operation and troubleshooting of Google Play Billing; they do not contain your vault files, passwords or photos. We may view sales, refund and settlement records made available in Play Console to handle order questions, reconcile revenue and meet applicable accounting or tax obligations. Google handles its transaction and diagnostic records under Google's policies. We cannot delete records held in your Google account or Google's Billing diagnostic systems on your behalf, and the app does not provide a single control to delete records held by third-party services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Local Wi-Fi migration. When you start a transfer, Ko Vault creates a password-protected encrypted backup and sends it over a local Wi-Fi connection to the device you select. The backup content is encrypted. Connection tokens, file-size information and receipt acknowledgments are protocol metadata and are not protected by HTTPS/TLS. Do not assume that every part of the local connection is protected in the same way as the encrypted backup. Use devices and networks you trust.

Exchange-rate requests use HTTPS, and Google Play Billing uses Google's billing connection. You can continue using the local vault without updating exchange rates, supporting the developer or checking orders. The app currently displays no ads and has no developer-operated cloud vault sync. Website and privacy-policy links open in your browser; the website host and browser may process information needed for those network requests. Local storage does not mean the app never connects to the internet.

4. Backups, retention and deletion

Items you delete in the app move to the recycle bin for seven days so you can restore them. After that period, they expire and are removed when the app runs its cleanup. You can permanently delete them earlier. You can also manage local content through the app or by clearing app data in Android settings. Clearing data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove local content.

Android automatic app backup is disabled. You decide whether to create an external backup, where to save it, whether to copy or share it, and when to delete it. Backups created by Ko Vault are password-protected and encrypted. Exported backups and other copies may remain after you delete the in-app content or uninstall the app; delete those copies separately at their destinations.

If you save or upload a backup to a cloud drive, NAS or other storage service that you choose, that provider handles the copy under its own terms and privacy policy. Keep your device, backup password and backup files safe. Without a usable backup, local content lost through device loss, app-data clearing or uninstalling may be unrecoverable.

Retention and deletion of records held by third-party technical or payment services are controlled by those providers and applicable requirements. Contact the relevant provider for requests concerning records it holds.

5. Sharing, clipboard and widgets

When you choose to export, share or open content in another app, the selected recipient or app receives the content you chose. Some export formats may not be encrypted. Text copied to the clipboard and information displayed in home-screen widgets may be accessible outside the vault's protection. Check the recipient and content before sharing. Third-party apps, storage providers and recipients handle the information they receive under their own terms.

6. Information you send when contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request. Please do not send your vault PIN, account passwords or unnecessary sensitive files. Your email provider may process information needed to transmit and store the message. You may contact us to ask about or request deletion of information we received in your email. Vault content stored only on your device must be managed on that device.

7. Policy updates

If app features or data-handling practices change, we will update this page and its last-updated date. Please review the current policy when using relevant features.
