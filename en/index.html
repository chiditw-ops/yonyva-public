02 / PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Clear security details, not big promises

Understand what's protected and when optional services connect.

Encrypted storage

Vault content in private app storage is encrypted using AES-256-GCM, with keys protected by Android Keystore.

Access on your terms

A local PIN controls access. Supported devices can use Android biometric verification; the app does not receive biometric templates.

No developer cloud sync

No Ko Vault cloud account or developer-operated vault sync. Android automatic app backup is disabled.

03 / IN YOUR HANDS

Choose when and where to back up

Create a password-protected encrypted backup and choose where to save or share it. Local Wi-Fi migration can transfer an encrypted backup to another device, although transport protocol metadata is not entirely protected by HTTPS/TLS.

Deleted in-app items remain in the recycle bin for seven days and are removed during app cleanup after expiry. Delete exported backups separately where you saved them.

04 / TRANSPARENT CONNECTIONS

Optional connections, only when needed

Local-first does not mean the app never uses the network. These actions are initiated by you.

Exchange rates

When you manually request an update, the app uses HTTPS to reach ExchangeRate-API or a custom HTTPS provider. That service may receive network information including your IP address.

Developer support

Optional, repeatable one-time purchases through Google Play. No subscription or feature unlock. Billing and order checks connect to Google Play.

Sharing & display

Exporting, sharing, copying to the clipboard or using home-screen widgets may expose selected content outside the vault.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No Ko Vault cloud account is required. Personal vault content is kept on your device.

Is my PIN the encryption password?

No. The PIN controls app access, while Android Keystore protects the encryption keys for local vault storage.

Can I recover data after uninstalling?

Uninstalling, clearing app data or losing your device may permanently remove local content without a usable encrypted backup. Keep your backup and password safe.
